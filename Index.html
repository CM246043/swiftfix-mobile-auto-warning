“Verify Before You Hire” – Auto Repair Checklist
Consumer checklist

The following checklist is intended to help consumers reduce risk and confusion when selecting a mobile auto repair provider or any auto service business.

  • Confirm the legal name: Ask for the exact legal business name and compare it to state business registry records.
  • Check the address: Verify that the address on invoices, websites, and registry filings match.
  • Review ownership information: Look up the owner or managing member in public filings and confirm consistency.
  • Search multiple sources: Use search engines, BBB, and state registries to cross-check the business name and owner.
  • Read recent reviews: Focus on recent, detailed reviews that clearly describe services, locations, and dates.
  • Ask for written estimates: Request a written estimate and keep copies of all invoices and communications.
  • Verify licensing (if applicable): Confirm any required local or state licenses for auto repair work.
  • Know your complaint options: Familiarize yourself with BBB, AG, and local consumer protection channels before hiring.
Reminder:
This checklist is general consumer guidance and is not specific to any single business. It is intended to help consumers make informed decisions and to reduce confusion when multiple businesses share similar names.
