2020 – 2021: AZ Mobile Auto Repair

  • January 1, 2020 — "Business Started" date for AZ Mobile Auto Repair (Glendale) on its BBB profile. Owner: Mr. Jordan Brunner.
  • August 11, 2020 — BBB opens its file on the business.
  • October 25, 2021 — Jordan Brunner registers the trade name "AZ Mobile Auto Repair" with the Arizona Secretary of State (5407 N 45th Dr, Glendale).

2022 – 2024: complaints pile up

  • November 2022 – June 2023 — 1-star Yelp reviews describe overcharging, missing parts and refused refunds.
  • February 2023 – June 2024 — BBB complaints describe upfront payments for work never done, parts marked up far above cost, and a missing original part and key. Each is marked "Unanswered."
  • June 9, 2023 — the trade name "AZ MOBILE AUTO" is filed with the Arizona Corporation Commission.

2025: the record starts to change

  • December 5, 2025 — the same Yelp page shows only 1 "not recommended" review.
