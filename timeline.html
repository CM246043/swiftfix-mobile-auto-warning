Public Record Timeline

This timeline tracks the public-facing business trail and the warning signal associated with the same owner.

1. Warning profile appears

AZ Mobile Auto Repair becomes the public-facing business name associated with Jordan Brunner. Public complaint history and a non-accredited business profile give the business a visibly negative warning signal.

2. Alternate business name appears

The same business pattern is later shown under the alternate name AZ Mobile Auto, with a distinct public profile. This matters because it shows the same owner and related business identity resurfacing behind a different public-facing label.

3. New business entity is formed

Swift Fix Mobile Auto LLC is formed and begins operating under a new business wrapper. The public-facing review signal does not always line up cleanly with the older warning history, especially when the ownership trail shows continuity.

4. Public complaints and legal record remain relevant

  • Public complaint history and prior negative BBB displays are still relevant to consumer judgment.
  • Public record and legal docket references tie the same individual to documented court and complaint exposure.
  • Risk should stay attached to the underlying owner and ownership trail, not disappear simply because a new business name is used.

5. Why the public signal matters

The consumer problem is not the business name alone. It is the gap between the warning signal and the underlying ownership continuity. When a business can soften its public-facing warning while the same owner remains connected, the public trust signal becomes unreliable.
