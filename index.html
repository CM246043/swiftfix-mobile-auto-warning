Swift Fix Mobile Auto reviews — and the BBB record

Swift Fix Mobile Auto's Google listing shows a perfect 5.0 stars — from exactly two reviews, both posted within weeks of the LLC's formation in August 2026. One comes from an employee or associate, and the other is too thin to establish a meaningful pattern of customer trust.

Compare the Better Business Bureau record for the same owner's prior business:

  • BBB lists Jordan Brunner as owner of AZ Mobile Auto Repair (Glendale) — not accredited, rated B- for failure to respond to a complaint. Its customer reviews are limited and the complaint history is not strong.
  • BBB previously displayed a second profile for this business under its alternate name, AZ Mobile Auto (Anthem), rated F: 11 complaints filed, failure to respond to those complaints.
  • BBB's pages also displayed the Glendale business itself at F (10 complaints) and D- (3 complaints) during 2026 — today it shows B- with a single complaint on the surface. That change is what consumers should not accept without explanation.

The pattern

AZ Mobile Auto Repair (2020, BBB previously a fail) → AZ Mobile Auto (BBB F, 11 complaints) → Swift Fix Mobile Auto LLC (August 2026). Same owner, different business wrapper. The public warning signal is fragmented and not clearly tied to the same underlying ownership story.

Why the pattern matters: the business names change, but the public-risk trail does not always stay connected. When complaint visibility and rating status change while the same owner keeps resurfacing under related names, consumers are being shown a managed impression rather than a clear and traceable risk signal.
