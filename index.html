Swift Fix Mobile Auto reviews — and the BBB record

Swift Fix Mobile Auto's Google listing shows a perfect 5.0 stars — from exactly two reviews, both posted within weeks of the LLC's formation in August 2026. One appears to come from someone connected to the business, and two reviews are too few to show a track record.

Compare the Better Business Bureau record for the same owner's prior business:

  • BBB lists Mr. Jordan Brunner, Owner for AZ Mobile Auto Repair (Glendale), with AZ Mobile Auto listed as an alternate name. Not accredited.
  • June 2025: BBB rated AZ Mobile Auto Repair F — "Failure to respond to 10 complaint(s)."
  • May 23, 2026: BBB rated it D- — "Failure to respond to 3 complaint(s)."
  • A separate BBB profile under the alternate name AZ Mobile Auto (Anthem) was rated F — "Failure to respond to 11 complaint(s)."
  • Today: the Glendale profile shows B- with a single complaint. The earlier ratings and complaints no longer show on the surface — consumers deserve to know they existed.

Complaints filed with BBB in 2023 and 2024 describe paying upfront for parts and work that were never delivered, parts being marked up far above cost, and a missing original part and vehicle key. On Yelp, AZ Mobile Auto Repair had 30 reviews "not recommended" (hidden from the star rating) in August 2025; by December 2025 Yelp showed only one.

All of this is documented by dated screenshots in the evidence section below.

The pattern

AZ Mobile Auto Repair (started 2020; BBB F, 10 complaints) → AZ Mobile Auto (trade name filed June 2023; BBB F, 11 complaints) → Swift Fix Mobile Auto LLC (August 2026). Same owner, different business name. The Arizona trade name registration for "AZ Mobile Auto Repair" expired on October 25, 2026.

Why the pattern matters: each new name starts with a clean slate, so the complaint history doesn't follow the owner. In my opinion, when ratings improve and complaints drop out of view while the same owner keeps reappearing under new names, consumers are seeing a cleaned-up picture rather than the full record.

Everything on this page comes from public records and dated screenshots. Check them directly:

Before paying anyone for mobile auto repair: confirm the exact legal business name, check the owner in state filings, read the complaint history — and get the estimate in writing.
